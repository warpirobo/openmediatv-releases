# Arios TV 0.15.27

- My torrents: account-scoped SQLite index, search, cached poster identification, availability and quality filters, with no manual pages. Indexing is progressive in batches of 50, up to 10,000 summaries with a limit warning.
- Separate resolution filters (4K/UHD, 1080p, 720p, SD, undeclared) and edition/capture filters (IMAX, CAM, other).
- Graphical TorBox seasons and episodes using TMDB posters/stills when available.
- Contained vertical focus within TorBox; Left and Back remain available.
- PlavioHD direct HTTPS movie/series provider, outside TorBox cache. No TorBox credentials shared; live-sports catalogs are not imported.
- Two configurable HTTPS addon slots. Streaming websites are not automatically treated as compatible addons.
- Redundant player-panel header buttons removed; remote Back dismisses panels.

Android TV / Fire TV, armeabi-v7a, Android 7+. Update without uninstalling.
Public APK excludes private TMDB/TheTVDB registrations and retains those previously
saved by a bridge version. Clean installations require your own provider keys.
Compilation and signing verified; no functional or playback tests performed.
Native libraries and backup limits unchanged. Arios TV application source remains private.
