# Arios TV 0.15.32

- Improve Spanish online subtitle discovery with HispaSubs and visible Latin American, Castilian and neutral dialect labels. Availability, classification and timing depend on the provider and matching subtitle release.
- Add secure local QR setup for supported optional providers. Pair from a phone or PC on the same private network, review the local certificate fingerprint and confirm on the TV. Explicit consent is required before sharing credentials with an external provider.
- Bound pairing sessions and requests, validate the TV PIN and browser origin, and keep saved configurations encrypted on the device. No credentials are included in QR codes or diagnostic messages.
- Preserve embedded subtitle priority, manual track choices, existing source configuration and native playback components, including multichannel audio and libass.

TV **armeabi-v7a**, Android 7 / API 24 or later. Update without uninstalling to retain your preferences and saved registrations.

Only the public APK is attached. Private TMDB/TheTVDB credentials are not included; new installations need their own registrations. Application source remains private.

Validation: successful release compilation, eight automated configuration/local-TLS tests, phone-form script checks, signing verification and native-library comparison. Public subtitle metadata was queried without account credentials. Personal-account linking and TV playback still require device verification.
